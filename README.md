Solmaris Heat is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. You play Mara Voss, a driver and fixer-for-hire in the sun-baked port city of Solmaris, taking jobs from the fixer Dario Quint and crossing the Ledger crew who run the docks. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, weave through live traffic and pedestrians, build up a wanted level and shake the police — cruisers, roadblocks, spike strips and a searchlight helicopter — and work through an 8-mission story of thefts, deliveries, tails, races and shootouts. Drive taxi fares, win street races, hunt hidden packages, and save your progress.

The city lives on a day/night cycle with golden-hour light, bay rain that slicks the roads, sun shadows that swing with the clock, and a full HUD with minimap, wanted stars and objectives. Every sound — engine, sirens, rain, rotor, music — is synthesized live with WebAudio; there are no assets and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser (any static file server works too).

## Controls

- **WASD** — move / drive
- **Shift** — sprint (stamina)
- **E** — steal or enter a car · talk · use
- **Mouse** — aim · **Click** — shoot
- **F** — punch · **Q** — swap weapon (buy the scattergun at Iron & Oak)
- **T** — start a taxi fare · **Space** — handbrake / drift
- **M** — city map · **Esc** — pause
